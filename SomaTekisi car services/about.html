Our History

Founded to address the growing demand for reliable, professional vehicle maintenance and repair services tailored to high-mileage taxi operators, commercial fleets, and private commuter vehicles. SomaTekisi has built a reputation for minimizing vehicle downtime through fast, quality mechanical servicing, diagnostic checks, and preventative maintenance.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To deliver prompt, transparent, and affordable vehicle repair and maintenance solutions that keep commuter transportation running safely and reliably.

Vision: To become the region's leading and most trusted automotive service partner for taxi owners, fleet managers, and individual motorists through modern diagnostics and service excellence.
